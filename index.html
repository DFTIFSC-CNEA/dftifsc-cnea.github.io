Department of Theoretical Physics of Fundamental Interactions and Complex Systems

We are the Theoretical Physics Department of the Gerencia de Investigación y Aplicaciones (GIyA) of the Argentine National Atomic Energy Commission (CNEA), based at the Centro Atómico Constituyentes in Buenos Aires. Our research ranges from quantum chaos, open quantum systems and complex networks to hadronic matter under extreme conditions, quantum field theory, nuclear physics, quantum liquids and atomistic simulation of materials.

Research

Quantum chaos and complex systems

Semiclassical methods and quantum chaos, open and dissipative quantum maps, scrambling and out-of-time-order correlators, phase-space representations, quantum information, and the Google matrix and dynamics of complex networks.

Hadronic physics and dense matter

Effective models of QCD (NJL, nonlocal chiral quark models), meson properties and the phase diagram of quark matter at finite temperature, density and strong magnetic fields, and applications to compact stars.

SFMIExplore →

Condensed matter, field theory and nuclear physics

Computational materials physics, quantum liquids, quantum field theory and the quantum Hall effect, and nuclear structure and reactions.
